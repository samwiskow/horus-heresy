Checked on 5 October 2026. The downloaded source SVG matches the saved SHA-256.
Its embedded document is Daunt's version 0.9, dated 2 July 2019.

The catalogue has 195 entries: the existing 62 plus 133 source records. The
Reference map represents 173 source nodes and 192 resolved arrows. The two
unattached endpoints are unchanged. The publisher's Saga and Siege lists are
unchanged and remain separate reading options.

## Work types and source labels

The source key identifies novels, short stories, exclusive unnumbered stories,
audio dramas, and graphic novels. It groups some novellas with short stories.
The app preserves that category separately from the work type. It uses the
publisher's publication record for the longer prose works listed below.
An audiobook reading does not change a novel or novella into an audio drama.

The complete reference has 41 novels, 18 novellas, 99 short-story appearances,
12 audio dramas, one graphic novel, and two collections. The existing two Siege
novellas remain in My library; they are outside the 2019 reference. New records
do not invent faction labels or plot summaries.

The Honoured and The Unburdened have exclusive unnumbered source shapes.
Their standalone publisher pages describe them as novels; the publisher's
Novella Collection 7 also includes them. The app uses the explicit standalone
product labels and preserves the source descriptions. Tallarn: Ironclad uses
the current publisher's novella label. These choices do not alter any arrows.

## Publications and completion

Collection numbers in source labels are metadata, not the individual story's
series number. Book notes link to existing collection entries or to the source's
publication link for Books 48, 50, and 52. These three volumes are not added as
map nodes: the reference depicts their individual works. Garro's related works
use “Adapted in” because the publisher identifies Book 42 as a novelisation,
not an anthology. The existing Garro entry is corrected to Novel.

The source's eBook and exclusive descriptions are historical labels. They do
not claim present availability. Publication record links support type decisions.
Finishing a collection never marks its individual works finished, and reading
position is preserved when another entry is finished.

## Map arrangement

The source snapshot now retains each node's original bounds. The full map uses
those centres with additional space for the 220 × 110 cards, expanding the
horizontal distance by 2.75 and the vertical distance by 4. This preserves the
original relative branch positions and gives the overview a mainly vertical
shape. Novels only retains exactly the same positions for its visible works.
Arrows retain their source endpoints; their bends are recalculated to avoid the
larger cards. Focus connections keeps its compact local layout.

## Views

All stories is the default. Novels only is a visibility filter across the same
reference graph and applies to map and list. Source next steps remain available;
opening a hidden story returns to All stories. A selected novel with hidden
outgoing steps has a Show all stories notice. No replacement arrows are drawn.
Focus connections explicitly reveals direct neighbours, including supporting
works excluded from the full map. Saga and Siege retain publisher order.

Saved reading options and completion IDs survive reloads. The new option uses
`reference-novels`; existing `reference`, `saga`, and `siege` saves retain their
meaning. There is no storage-key change or silent collection expansion.
