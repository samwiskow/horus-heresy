# Siege of Terra route

Checked on 1 October 2026.

The [reference flowchart](https://www.kylebb.com/HH/HHSeriesOrder.svg) names
The Siege of Terra and connects The Buried Dagger to The Solar War.
Its July 2019 snapshot stops at The Solar War, with an unattached outgoing
arrow. That unresolved source record remains intact.

The continuation uses [Black Library's series catalogue](https://www.blacklibrary.com/series/series-sot)
and the book numbers on its product pages. The ten main volumes are books
1–7 and the three parts of book 8. Each product URL and number is recorded
in `src/sources/siege-of-terra.json`.

Explore ends with a Siege of Terra section that opens this separate reading
option. The source choice is explicit. It does not add guessed arrows to
Daunt's diagram or append books to the publisher's twelve-book Saga selection.

The route follows the numbered main series. It does not assign a position to
supporting novellas or anthologies. Those remain available through the
publisher's catalogue; existing supporting books remain in My library.

The list uses positions 01–10 and shows the publisher's book number beneath
each title, including book 8's three parts. All 62 catalogue IDs and saved
reading progress remain unchanged. The selected Siege option is saved too.
